# License

Kernos is released under the **Apache License 2.0**. The full text is in
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

## What that means for a company using it

You may run Kernos across your whole organisation, modify it, build commercial
products on it, and keep every change private. There is no obligation to publish
anything you write on top of it, and no obligation to share the workflow bundles
that describe how your company works.

Apache 2.0 rather than MIT for one reason that matters at company scale: it grants
you a **patent licence** from every contributor. A company's legal team will ask
about that, and MIT has no answer.

Apache 2.0 rather than AGPL for the opposite reason: AGPL would require a company
running Kernos as an internal service to publish its modifications. That is
exactly the outcome this project is designed to avoid. Your processes are yours.

## The boundary in one line

The **engine** is Apache 2.0 and public. The **bundles** you write on it are your
own work, under whatever terms you choose, and nothing in this licence reaches
them.
